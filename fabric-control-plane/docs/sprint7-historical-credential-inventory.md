1. **Approve scope and containment.** Incident owner signs the inventory and retention instructions. Platform and security owners restrict historical log, Helm, backup and artifact readers and preserve access records. Inventory each affected certificate using public DER and public-key fingerprints, serial, issuer, use, expiry, dependent channels and earliest/latest exposure. Keep suspected and confirmed items separate. Assign a named owner to each credential class.
2. **Approve rehearsal and recovery.** Fabric org and channel owners approve the authorized topology and capture the ledger/quorum baseline. Rehearse a disposable restore with protected storage and fresh approved identities. Exercise stale identity rejection and fresh transactions across each channel. Confirm image pins, controller ownership, storage identities and a written abort point for each change. Record fingerprints rather than key bodies.
3. **Prepare fresh material.** Enroll fresh keys in separate immutable bundles. Validate key-to-certificate match, chain, EKU, SANs, enrollment authority, expiry and channel membership before reference changes. Apply the approved public certificate and public-key denial lists to the candidate. Confirm issuer and controller access boundaries. If a CA root is implicated, qualify new roots and dependent identity migration before activating leaf replacements.
4. **Approve execution per class and channel.** Coordinate the one-writer controller handoff and rotate workload TLS, MSP signing, orderer admin TLS and Raft consenter material as distinct changes. Prepare and approve trust changes on each affected channel. Update consenters individually with a verified surviving majority, and check channel status and ledger readback after each. Stop on a quorum, endorsement, trust or storage mismatch. Before channel changes, any rollback bundle must be explicitly approved and unexposed. After channel trust or consenter changes, use a separately rehearsed Fabric forward-recovery procedure.
5. **Retire and close.** Revoke or remove the old identities and trust only after each dependency has switched and validation passes. Verify stale credentials are rejected, fresh transactions commit on the expected peers, and ledger hashes agree. Verify secret-free logs and artifacts. Incident owner and independent reviewer sign the disposal or retention of historical artifacts and the final incident receipt.

Owner inputs needed are the restricted collector/export inventory, affected issuance windows and public certificate inventory, approved rehearsal topology and recovery evidence, signed maintenance scope, and per-channel quorum and rollback approvals.
